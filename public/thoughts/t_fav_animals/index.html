Some Of My Favorite Animals/Birds/Insects yada yada - Fri, Oct 4, 2024

I love almost all creatures on this planet of ours(almost)

I love animals. Yeah no surprise if you know me. Animals have always interested me because we humans have evolved thinking, these animals don’t so the question of how do they live and all is very interesting to me. Another thing is I love variety in life and these animals come in all shapes and sizes with various different idk quirks? Whatever the sheer variety is another thing that draws me in.

I guess my favorite animal of all time are dogs. Fair take eh? A dog is never selfish. Being with a dog also helped me understand what unconditional love is.

Back when I was young(1st grade) we had adopted a pair of street pups whose mother had unfortunately passed away. I was young and dumb and thought why does this dog want to live with us. Another thing is I was scared of it. So I never really gave it a lot of attention. Occasional head pats when my family would play with it or just calling its name out to see if it recognizes or not was all I did to it.

One night after returning from a fairly long party I was really tired and wanted sleep. Browny, concerned that we were not at home for such a long time, was elated when he saw us returning. It ran up to me and rubbed himself on by legs and tried to that dog affection jump on me. I just scared and pushed it aside. My mom rightfully reprimanded me. She said “what was that for?” , I said it was attacking me to which mom told me “no beta it was trying to love you”. Love? ME? A person who barely even interacts with the dog? I was genuinely puzzled.

In the following days I tried to spend more time with the lil pooch and his brother. Things were starting to get better and I was starting to form a bond with the dog. Unfortunately one day I was playing with rocks. You know just throwing them up and seeing when it will fall down. Dumb me threw it too high, I didn’t know where it will fall so I ran away. My doggy was just chilling around and walking and what happened next still scars me to this day. The stone fell on my dog. Poor chap started whining in pain and limped away to my dad who was sitting there. He was so disappointed he didn’t say anything to me. just gave me a look, and just started helping the dog out.

One of the worst feelings came to me. Some animal decided to love me without expecting anything in return and I wound it? What kind of a monster was I. My mom came rushing our hearing the dog’s cries but I was too ashamed of myself and ran in and began to cry. I apologized to my parents and what could they even say they just said don’t do it again. I didn’t see my dog that night. I was just too dead inside to even show up infornt of the dog. I realized I had to make things work back with the Browny, It was my mistake and I had to do something about it. The next day I remember asking my grandpa to take me out to the local store to buy a pack of Parle’-G. lil me thought the best way to apologize was by giving the other person something to eat.

I see my dog curled up in the garden, resting after what had happened the day before. So I sat next to it. And started talking to it(lil me read a lot of panchatantra). Started saying stuff like I am sorry galti hogayi blah blah. Before I could even finish speaking and give him the biscuits he woke up, started to wag his tail and tried to lick my face. The dog had already forgiven me. This destroyed me, I started sobbing profusely and hugged the man’s best friend.

This one incident made me permanently fall in love with dogs, and also taught me about the joy in loving someone unconditionally, forgetting one’s self and living in the present and being happy.

Even besides this incident being with dogs just make me really happy. They are such happy little things, warms my heart. I started reading a little dog psychology too so that I can interact better with dogs.

So yeah dogs are my favorite animals to date and I don’t see this changing any time soon.

Now for birds. I like birds a lot. Elegant creatures, flying in the vast blue sky, being mobile and still having dexterity despite having only two legs and the lovely chirps they make(there are some exceptions).

But unlike dogs, just saying I love birds wont do it justice ill have to be a bit more specific.

My number 1 favorite bird has to be swans. Sorry duck lovers but I just like swans more! Looking at swans gives me a sense of peace. Swans are often associated with light, love, wisdom, poetry and melancholy passion and boy I vibe with that shit. As an XNFP I am supposed to have two spirit animals and the INFP side of me resonates with swans(the ENFP goes with dolphins). Swans are very calm yet social birds. They are not violent, they do not cause chaos, and live in harmony. Swans are extremely romantic too, once they find a mate they stick with them forever(is it true for me tho? lol). Also they are a symbol of hard work too, a duck appears stationary on the surface but underneath it is constantly paddling its feet. Just so good cant get enough of swans.

Besides the swan I have an odd soft spot for spotted doves. No not the generic peace sign white dove(love them loads too tho), the ones you would find near your house with a cream-ish color and you guessed it, spots. These look very different from your regular doves but they still have the same symbolism - peace, truth, and emotional expression. I said I love the sounds birds make, love the sound this bird makes a lot! People often compare this bird with pigeons but come on man this is beautiful and thats lowkey ugly and dont even get me started on the noise pigeons make(plus they shit so much oh my fucking god). I have fed spotted doves before but never managed to get close enough to touch one ;-; maybe some day.

another bird i’d give a mention is the blue jay commonly known as the Mordecai bird. IT HAS THE BEST SOUND A BIRD CAN MAKE. Plus it has a very lovely shade of blue. It symbolizes intelligence and trickery. Food for thought - if you get a dream with a blue jay in it, it often is a warning to something.
