Stoicism And Why I Follow It - Wed, Mar 2, 2022

Sigma jokes aside it's a good outlook on life ya know

Till a certain age in my life I had been living life in a manner that made no sense. I would do whatever would come to my head and had almost 0 rationality, only emotional decision making. AND as person who had massive mood swings this was not the best thing out there. So at some point I decided that I need to stop being a slave to my emotions.

I am not sure what and how i did it, but i managed to improve my emotional strength but at the same time managed to get some control over my emotions. I thank my investment into stoicism for this.

The modern day stoic is said to be a person who remains calm under pressure and avoids emotional extremes. (yes i go thorugh my phases too but only during my me time). However the original philosophy is more than just an attitude. It says while we may not have control over the things affecting us, we can have control over how we approach things. Rather than imagining the ideal society, the stoic tries to deal with world the way as it is.

The way we deal with world is by self improvement using four cardinal values called the pillars. The first being 1. wisdom, practical wisdom the ability to navigate complex situation in a logical, informed and calm manner. 2. Temperance , the exercise of self restraint and moderation in all aspects of life. 3. Justice, treating others with fairness even when they have done wrong. 4. Courage, not just in extreme scenarios but facing daily challenges with clarity and integrity. Most if not all people who have been with me in the past few years will agree upon these virtues being in me.

Now stoicism does focus on self improvement but it is not only selfish. It says that we all are humans, and we all are similar so we all can improve. So it does say try spreading the knowledge of stoicism, and the positivity you gained from it. The idea is that those who have cultivated enough virtue and self-control in them can bring positive change to others.

Now i dont follow this a 100%, i try to follow it and am more or less successful in doing so. p.s some concepts of stoicism are a bit Bullshit cuz like one chap said the true victory in life is achieved when you end it. So he promotes suicide. :| I do not promote suicide. Live life man there is so much to appreciate and experience.
