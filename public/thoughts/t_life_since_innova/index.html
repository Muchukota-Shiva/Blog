End Of Unemployment

But yes for updates i guess as the title suggests, I have a job now. After months of feeling hollow worrying about placements career and all that i finally have a job.


Well it is good that I have a job and it is better than my internship(CRED paid me handsomely but it took probably 5 years of my lifespan with the amount of work and stress I had), but it still doesnt feel very nice. Let me break down the good and bad.

first to start off, here is a comparison of me around the start of college and me a lil recently. something out of the mandela catalogue for sure. Anyways lets begin

who is this beautiful creature <3
So its nice that I have financial independence now kinda. Gone are the days of me having a 2k per month allowance(yes when I used to say I am a low maintenance kid i meant it). Now I can spend with no guilt. Now i have not gone berserk, i still spend carefully, i do buy watches and perfumes every now and then but i keep a check on it.

Thats one good thing, besides this I guess my career has started. Got a job for an AI role. One of my biggest concerns used to be what if AI taks my job this that bullshit, but now I work with AI so i am on the right path. Its not a 100% secure cuz the CSE job market is a bitch like that but this is better than being easily replaceable like a back-end developer(cough cough lowkey got replaced by AI in cred as a back-end dev cough). Also to clarify I am working on good side of AI, to automate tedious stuff not the generative tiktok AI brainrot slop.

Lastly for the goods I guess there is a routine now. I work a 11-8/9 shift. So i get up, do whatever for 3-4 hours till 11 then work, eat, sleep. In CRED there were no fixed working hours which at first seemed like a good thing. No fixed hours did mean I could start at any time, but it also meant I couldn’t choose when to end. You all know how that went with my whole clinical insomnia mishap and all that. Here, if my working hours are over, im good baby good bye.

Now lets start with the cons. CSE jobs kinda suck lol. As a kid I wanted to be either a biologist or a pilot. But then since 10th class I started doing well in computer science, and I felt damn this shit fine let me focus more on this. CSE was booming at that time, during lockdown my mother got a remote job which gave her 1.2 lakhs a month, as a non cs graduate. I got enticed. Plus my teachers kept saying you are in the top students of the school for computer sciences, go ahead into this field. Then came lockdown. This field got FUCKED. Oversaturated. Now the job market is worse than entering the market with a diploma. Saw a job the other day which asked for 2 years experience as a java dev and the pay was 3 lpa or something. Just become a farmer at this point vro. Coming back to the point, its a rat race. The job isnt exactly fun. It was fun learning this and all but the actual practice from day to day is just dookie. That and how everyone in the world knows that indian tech culture is just more toxic than every where else lmfao so i wont explain this any more.

Next is a continuation of the schedule i mentioned. Its cool and all but there is not much i do now. I gotta sit my ass down for a major part of the day. I am a hyperactive lil shit i need to move around and all, sitting duck all day keeps me up at night. I have been squeezing in some time to exercise lately but i wish i could do some more.

Have been applying for places outside of tech tho. Got shortlisted for indian army, air force and navy(hopefully coast gaurd soon too) 🧿. I wont be able to give the SSB for all cuz i wont get that much leave but if i get into any of these i am quitting tech for the SSC period of time. But baad ki baat baadme no khayali pulao here. Have been preparing for SSB on the weekends, hopefully i do well pray for me folks 🧿.

MBTI Change

for the longest time I have been a part of the diplomats section of the MBTI types.

for those who dont know there are 4 broad types(unofficially)

  • • analysts - known for their impartiality, rationality and intelectual experience
  • • diplomats - known for their empathy, diplomatic skills and passionate idealism
  • • sentinels - known for their practicality and focus on order, stability and security
  • • explorers - known for their spontanuity, ingenuity and flexible nature.

So as I said, i was in the diplomats section. Specifically as an ENFP. Recently since my final year in college ive had a shift in personality and maturity and hence I have a change. So this is what my MBTI is now. ENTJ

This is quite a shift. The "feeling" and "prospecting" parts of my personality have shifted to "thinking" and "judging" respectively. It is pretty much on the edge, very close to the 50% mark, so its not like i am some new sigma yogurt guy or something now, but something has changed. If I look at it, it adds up. Working and living in a city alone is quite a responsibility. I think even before this, I started realizing that the job market is bad and I need to lock the fuck in or else the future wont be bright. Things like this kinda pushed the personality change.

Not Much Time For Video Games

As you would expect, I have a fucking job now, so leisure time is reduced. So even though I have massive backlog of unfinished video games along with a few newer games(a few, modern games suck lol) I do not have time to play those.

Might sound hypocritical, but I play dota and deadlock almost every day though. The other games I have are very lets say, engaging. They demand you to sit down with you controller for probably hours to get something done. Not saying its a bad thing, but its for those who have time and im broke in that regard.

Dota and deadlock are multiplayer games which dont save progress between sessions. You log in, play, leave. its like a roguelike game but multiplayer. Another thing to like is that it has enough complexity to scratch my brain and keep me invested and give me enough dopamine to keep me wanting to play again.

The concept of picking a team of 5 heroes out of 100+ heroes, against the 5 they choose, and then going at it is always fresh. What I mean is there is no strict outcome you can expect. Take COD BO Zombies. It has a fixed path. You do not have much scope for creativity in there. Re playable? sure but you can get tired of it. Dota on the other hand has just so much in it no match can ever feel the same. For scale here is an image of all the characters in the game.

Each character is good at something and is countered by someone. 5V5 here can take any route, especially considering you can ban your opponents from picking some characters.

So yeah been like this fo a while now. Still working, still gaming, still yapping, still nerding out.
