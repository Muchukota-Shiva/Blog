the big 5 types

now this is an old quiz, and the website i gave it on also does not work anymore so thats a bummer anyways We were asked to do a quiz based on the ‘big five personality traits’. These are actually 10 types, but each person gets 1 out of pairs of two. The first is how open we are. We can either be closed or open minded. Then we have conscientiousness, where we can either be organized or disorganized. Next we have how social we are with introversion or extroversion. Next is how agreeable we are, like if we can understand other people and agree to them, the pairs here are agreeable and disagreeable. Lastly we have neuroticism, how negative emotionally we are, here we can either be calm or nervous.

I Agree with these results. I am a very open minded person, I like exploring new possibilities and I am open to change. I also am not quick to judge people. I am a very organized person, I like having things in order and maintaining a to- do list at times(which is why you might find me scribbling away in my pocket diary at times.) Next I am a very agreeable person, very true. I am a very empathetic person and this, at least I feel so, has been a great strength of mine. I always try to see things from the perspective of the other person before saying or doing something. This agreeability, paired with my open-minded nature and ability to be a good listener make me a very amiable person. Next I am somewhere in the middle of introversion and extroversion, a little more to the side of introversion. I am not so shy that I cant talk to people and not extroverted enough to just walk up to someone and start a conversation. I generally am more on the introverted side to the general public, but around friends when I am in my comfort zone I tend to become quite extroverted. Lastly we have negativity of emotion. I do not wish to but will have to agree this is true. As a person who tends to think on a deeper level a lot, and think about outcomes of various situations I feel it is natural that I tend to feel a bit negative because the most desirable outcome is always one of many, and with more possibilities the possibility of the desired outcome reduces.. I am a very optimistic and happy person but at times when I am alone I feel very sad and negative. We all have heard the joke the class clown laughs in school but cries at home. I am fine though I have strong will power and I get out of these high low mood swings of mine.

So these were the results of my quiz, I am happy to get more insight about myself.

Enneagram types

This is a test I had never heard of before and I honestly liked it a lot too :D so basically like we have 16 mbti we have 9 enneagrams. I gave the test and got a type 2 closely followed by type 4.

Type 2 people are called the helpers, caring individuals who help others reach their potentials. We see the world like “I will help the world out the way I can, in return I will receive their approval and love”. We typically tend to be supportive to others, generate good feelings and are very kind and generous HOWEVER we often over focus on being relationships too much and try avoiding conflict in fear of disapproval. Another shortcoming is that we dont let others know our needs but still expect them to meet them. We fear being unwanted or being unloved but who doesnt lov us :3

Type 4 people are called romantics. Fours are self-aware, sensitive, and reserved. They are emotionally honest, creative, and personal, but can also be moody and self-conscious. Withholding themselves from others due to feeling vulnerable and defective, they can also feel disdainful and exempt from ordinary ways of living. They typically have problems with melancholy, self-indulgence, and self-pity. At their Best they are inspired and highly creative, they are able to renew themselves and transform their experiences. These people might seem like goths or depressed people and low-key are because they are having inner conflicts. One fear of theirs is that they feel that they have no personal significance but in fact have very strong identities, just dont know how to express it.
