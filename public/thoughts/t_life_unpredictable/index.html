Life Does Not Always Go How You Want It To - Thu, Jul 23, 2026

I make good plans, then life looks at my plan and goes yeah do the opposite

This post is a lil bit of a brain fart cuz im rambling. Do not mind the lack of cohesion thanks 😽

As meticulous as I am, I often play it by the ear. Having said that one might ask how can someone be a well planned person but spontaneous at the same time. That is because most of my plans reach their end, something or the other happens and I need to change course of my actions. And no this does not make me bad at planning as lets say 75% of times things actually are just how i planned it, the whole point of this blog of mine is to express how not everything in life does not go as per your wishes

I guess the a significant example of this that I an think of is that during my early high school I wanted to be in the sports team for sports day. And sports day usually happens towards the winter and you guys might recall this is around the time I was crippled with my sinusitis issue. [for those who don't know Sinusitis is like having a heavy, throbbing pressure trapped behind your face]. I was selected for the annual day march past and was going to go for basketball trials as well but with my sinusitis it was extremely painful doing anything, even studying for the exams approaching was getting hard so I had to let go of that idea. After this the race for board exams higher studies etc started so extra curricular stuff was nowhere to be seen for me. But in the end i did manage to study well and get a good grade and in the improve my health.

But the point is not that boohoo nothing ever happens as i want it to blah blah , its that despite the uneven odds I manage to catch on the best possible string from the remaining cloth and hold on to it to make something better for myself.

Sure it is not always the best thing, take how I had to take graphic and gaming as my engineering specialization instead of AI/ML or CSF. It happened due to some records error in the college’s system which somehow didn’t register the fact I gave a preference for my course. Once everything was done they gave me a lil oops your record is lost you will have to take one of these remaining courses which is obviously not ideal in any way, also who makes the decision of what specialization you will have for your career be on a first-come-first-serve basis instead of merit? I had a 9.1 GPA i would’ve gotten anything. Either way i made the best of it. I still managed to get a good 8.51 cgpa overall, managed to meet some amazing people and have a good social life, became the student placement representative, learned some really interesting technologies like augmented and virtual reality, still studied AI/ML as a minor subject, still played sports and stuff and got exposure which i probably would not have gotten with the people in other batches.

Instead of giving more examples of mishaps ill say how i think of it mentally.

So i follow this aspect of Buddhism where you have no expectations for anything you do. The feeling of disappointment if something does not work out stems from the expectation that something you wanted will happen the way you wanted to, by not having an expectation you will be happy regardless of the outcome. This is not to be confused with having ambitions, because the expectation is that your hard work will result in something, the expectation here should be that your hard work should be plentiful and if it is not work harder!

You see what I am getting at? Have expectations for things that are IN YOUR CONTROL. Say you need to reach your friends house to meet up, why have expectations with how the visit will be? Instead focus on how you will reach, and how will present yourself and how you will behave, because that is something YOU CAN DO.

When things did go south i did not stop because instead of lamenting on the failure/stoppage I instead starting looking for other paths to continue ahead. My life has not been easy in any way but I persevered nonetheless.

gambhir samasya
Another thing that has plagued me for years is very clear ambiguity in my life. I never usually have a clear answer for some of the big decisions in life. Biggest example I can think of right now is the decision if I should go full throttle towards preparation for defense or just suck an egg and prepare for defense. For the past 2 years i've had this dilemma. I got jobs in corporate, first to be placed in college btw but I know i am better and can definitely be in the service like my father. Last 2 years i've been trying to balance both work life balance of corporate and you guys know how its been. Corporate is not stable enough, life is a rut, and lowkey i do not enjoy a sedentary life style. Defense on the other hand offers everything good, however entry into defense is no piece of cake. An all India difficult written exam, 5 day rigorous multi faceted interview with a 1 in 50 selection rate(many get screened out on day 1 itself), followed by strict medicals and finally a merit list cuz not everyone can fit in, the defense requires the best. I've been trying my best to balance both but its not working great. Now I have quit my job cuz they put me on the bench post project completion for too long. No regrets, got more time to study, actually take care of myself, connect with my beautiful friends again and a lot more.

I now stand having decided that I will give it my all this attempt, wont try to ride on two trains at once. Ill end up nowhere, like i sort of did right now. I am confident and I WILL make it.

This is one case where I have finally made a decision myself. Even one of my relatives, who is a professional astrologist, suggested that my life will be full of ambiguities. If I try to do multiple things at once I will end up in the middle, if I pick one and work hard for it I will achieve what I have head out for.

Good times are ahead of us twin. A step might look small but its better than not moving or worse going backwards

If you keep reading the same chapter you will never finish the book
