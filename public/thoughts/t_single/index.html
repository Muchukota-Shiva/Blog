Single, But Not Sorry(old and outdated) - Mon, Apr 18, 2022

Enjoy your own company

Love is all everyone wants to talk about. Conversation dying? just throw in a “hows your love life”, nothing to tease your friend about? pull this up. Honestly for me, saying my love life has been dull would be wrong because I never had one XD

So back to my pre fifth grade self. I was perfectly fine with girls my age. I was quite extroverted, and didn’t mind hanging out with girls. A big plus for me was that I have a twin sister, now she had a ton of female friends and if I was around my sister I had to be around them so I somehow got the hang of how to be with girls. I didn’t have a crush before 7th class(I was like a super super close friend of this girl in 5th but I don’t think we liked each other outside of that).

However once I came to 6th class, I went to a new school in the south. My general outgoing hang out with girls self wasn’t going to fly here. I realized this when I realized that this one friend of mine was getting a bit uncomfortable because of me. This made me feel horrible as a person. So i started distancing myself a little and started giving them more space. This was a good move because in early teenage you ought to give them some space, for come on man. This did result in them feeling, as quoted by them, “safe around me”. I felt this was quite a nice thing but this resulted in them calling me brother. I was perfectly fine with this. I did not feel the need for having a girlfriend, so being reliable enough that a girl calls you her brother was a big compliment for me.

In late 7th class however much to my surprise I had a crush on a girl in my tuition. She was in a different section but we would talk outside of tuition and sit together in class. Was a very nice person and was pretty too so I fell ._. this was probably if not in fifth the first time I fell I had a crush on someone(note that this was an infatuation). As this was the first time I was behaving like an idiot. I would become a mumbling idiot whenever she was around. Also she found a place in my head so I would if not always most of the time be thinking about her. I never confessed to her because I was a pussy scared of rejection lol. I decided to move on around mid semester of 8th class, when I started realizing I am wasting my time for what, some attention? some validation? So this is where I dropped the crush and moved on with a very woke mindset(begin 9th and post 9th period)

P.S if you wondering I never told her about my crush on her, but my friends would definitely have after I changed schools. Lmao we lost contact after it aaaand its too awkward to go back now

Now enter 9th class. My woke period was starting now. As we were in our late teens now I started distancing myself from girls even more because 1. we were in Delhi, unsafe place for women so I didn’t want any trouble and 2. I had forgotten how to talk to girls. Like seriously I realized that till now almost all of my female friends were mutual friends either from my sister or that one extrovert guy. In the end I did become fine talking to them slowly but I wanted it not to escalate from there because I had created this mindset that those who have girlfriends don’t do well academically. You cant blame me, most kids who I knew who had girlfriends were shit in studies so this is what I perceived, however this is balls. Oh right another thing was that I was not able to communicate with people outside of school, my society was not very social and this with me slowly turning introverted made me loose all social skills. I got some very nice friends but nothing more than that.

now 11th and 12th. New school again. My section literally had less than 10 girls. I was slowly opening up a bit more and gaining a bit of my extroversion back but by that time I had just acquainted the girls in class, and then came covid :0 oh lord. I was texting people so that’s good and all but face to face skills went to the bin. Once again my academics first social life later attitude was back to keep me single. So yeah nothing in 11th and 12th.

For me, I dont care what the other person looks like. Everyone looks good in their own way. What mattered to me was what they looked like from the inside. Looks are temporary but a heart is forever. Also being an INFP i am very choosy about who qualifies and all that. So even If i get close to people I never take it too far. This complemented my new lack of social skills. I am a deeply caring and affectionate person BUT I am not good at expressing it. So I think even if people try at me they feel I am not interested and move on. Also It seems I do not understand what hints are :)

Me in college now. I wont say it is peer pressure but the fact that literally every tom dick and harry is in a relationship got me wondering wait what. This thought and me being a bit lonely when college started made me wanting social validation and probably some love cuz people were not really nice to me at the start. So despite me trying to avoid this I had a crush again. However with my willpower I managed to get over it within a week : J

Now as things are, I don’t see myself with anyone anytime soon and I am fine with that. What will I get some extra attention? some extra affection but then there are the side affects like me giving this more attention than other things and I heard there are some rules to maintain trust and all like bruh think long term if you cant trust each other what in the future.

So yeah I am single and fine with that. I am fine having good friends. Am i going hunting now? no. If someone finds me after a hunt would I go? depends on the person.

Well people always say I jinx things all the time. SO saying I was single took away that too lol.

Very happy found the perfect person. And we both match each others wavelength and freak, so its perfect :)
