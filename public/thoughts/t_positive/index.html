Why So Positive? - Wed, Feb 19, 2025

People often ask me how I am as optimistic as I am despite all the bullshit that happens in my life

Most people when they first meet me they will either go “bleh quite kid” or “bruh how so happy”. I am quite the walking contradiction agreed but I like to think despite how depressed or whatever I am, I am quite the happy person. Sounds rubbish I know but hear me out.

I used to a super negative person. Like full on doomer. I would do bad things, be rude to people, be selfish etc. Then somewhere down the line my moral compass activated along with my empathy. I started realizing I would be pissed af if someone were to behave with me the way I behave with others.

Having changed, seeing people be like this reminds me of my younger self. I just cant bring myself to be like that anymore, something just feels wrong about it. From Buddhism I learned that the key to happiness is to not have expectations. I have reached a state where I can be nice to everyone without expecting the same in return. Sucks being the only good guy but then that is who I am now. It is not pity what compels me to be nice to them, I just feel everyone deserves some positivity from others no matter how negative they are, be it from me or anyone else.

Because I always used to be negative, I know what it is like to have something missing inside you, that urge that makes you what you are. I vowed to never feel like that again and help those who feel like that. I made it my mission to be a bright positive light in this dark grim selfish world. I must be kind. I must be strong. I must love. Not just for myself because then what would be the point.
