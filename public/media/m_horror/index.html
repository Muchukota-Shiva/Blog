Horror - Wed, Oct 1, 2025

Spooky scary skeletons send shivers down your spine

People who have known me before 9th grade would know that I WAS A MFING PUSSY when it came to horror. I would get scared of the smallest things. The doll my sister owns? yes, sleeping with lights off? yes, EVEN LOOKING AT THE IMAGE OF A GHOST WOULD KEEP ME UP AT NIGHT.

However now I relish horror. During horror movie nights while others are hiding in blankets and clutching each others hands I just sit and eat chips or something Its just not that scary anymore. I am quite happy with this change. I went from a horror avoiding wuss to a horror enjoyer. I was curious about how I just went complete 180 and so I sat out with my headphone like when I do when I wanna have some sweet self reflection and so here is what I thought.

Ever since I was young I had a very imaginative and creative mindset. So whenever I would see something I would get lost in my own world thinking about it. So naturally when young me(about 3rd grade) was exposed to horror elements my brain went bananas and I would have the strangest and most odd thoughts(which was noticed by some teachers the way I used to be scribbling in my notebooks and that one time the class was asked to draw a monster and I drew a horrific eldritch abomination which even spooked the teacher[comment for details on this one lol]). This mindset paired with a bunch of bullies who would aggravate the negativity would result in me getting shit scared.

Soon I went to mysore(6th-8th grade) and over there I met some people who loved horror. Now mysore had a small campus so it had lesser people, and if you didnt wanna do what they were doing you were out of luck. Now guess what all of them feel like doing every party when the parents aren’t around. Yes watch horror movies. I would be super scared. I was so scared I would start screaming even before a jumpscare(I hate jumpscares, more about that later). So now I had to do something about this right otherwise I would be the odd one out. This was also the time where I was exposed to more and more video games and then game the next phase.

I loved youtube and video games. Now i had to get used to horror somehow or the other. So the first thing I thought was If I gamify horror I will be getting what I want. But then I was too scared to playy horror games so I just would watch some youtubers play it and after a while I gained some courage! I started with some horror hybrids like some zombie shooters and thats how I was getting better and better. I saw some real change once I got into FNAF(ok the games are good its the community that is shit). FNAF would have some really good horror principles. It had tense atmosphere and was difficult enough to be fun and fair. Here I would get jumpscared a few times and then I would realize “Hey that wasnt that scary”. Now I was no longer scared of jumpscares. What were they? a second or two of shock? once the jumpscare its gone its just fine so this mindset was what got me more and more into horror.

Around 9th grade I started finding things like liminal art, horror artwork, creepy pastas, and more and more horror games. Some of my favorite games have to be Dead space(one of my inspiration to be a game dev), resident evil 7, fnaf 1,2,4, soma, the evil within and the plethora of Indie horror games. Video games in my opinion are probably the best form of media right now. Its not like videos where your involvement is minimal and all you can do is try to see things from the perspective of the protagonist. In case of video games you ARE the protagonist and your actions change the course of action so I feel this is the superior form of horror and indulging in this form made me happier viewing this. And like how playing dark souls made other forms of action feel lackluster, playing horror games made other things not feel the same.

The main source of horror I consume these days is psychological horror as it something quite tangible. It is not just oo spooky face or oo look how i dismember this person and they scream in agony. It plays with your mind, and thoughts about the experience linger in your head for days to come. An example of this would be the movie heridetary and the babadook; they do not have many scary moments as such but the movie lowkey disturbs you which is a new kick i found. Even in video games psychological horror games like soma or some of the silent hill games are mwah chef’s kiss.

But the games weren’t the only thing. I discovered, analog horror. quoted from wikipedia - “Analog horror is a niche sub genre of horror fiction and an offshoot of the “found footage” film technique. Often cited as originating from early 2010s Internet videos, analog horror is characterized by low-fidelity graphics, cryptic messages, and visual styles reminiscent of late 20th century television and analog recordings. Notable analog horror works include Local 58, Gemini Home Entertainment, and The Mandela Catalogue. " so this stuff first had my curiosity but quickly had my attention. One of the biggest strengths of type of horror is that It leaves a lot for you to imagine. It is ironic how the aspect of horror that scared me the most is now making me love horror :D . This is a rather hard section to explain so here is one of my favorite in the genre. keep your lights off, maximize the volume and let your mind run free. Once again this is a pretty niche sub genre of horror so dont be disappointed if this is not your cup of tea (man i really love me some niche stuff huh)

BOO be scared PISS YOURSELF

The other sort of horror I enjoy a lot is the ones where a community is involved. And these are not your typical horror where you have jump scares and atmosphere. These are text based with minimal imagery. And these are not made by one person, but as I said an entire community makes entries/content for it. Heck if I feel like it even I can add my own entry for it. I am talking about the backrooms project and the SCP foundation.

So the idea behind the backrooms is kinda cool. Like sometimes in old video games if you go around some corners you would fall or “no clip” out of the game boundaries. So similarly here it says if by any chance you no clip out of reality you will end up in the backrooms. The backrooms consists of various “levels”, each varying a lot. Each level has different locations, entities, hostility and ways to enter/exit. And this is where the community comes in. The community is in-charge of creating the levels and the entities that reside in it and try to make a story out of it. Some of my favorite levels(as of writing) are level 7-thallasophobia, level 1-habitable zone, level 974-kitty’s house, level 6.1-the snackrooms and so many more :D

SCP on the other hand is a fictional secret organization documented by the collaborative writing wiki project of the same name, with the abbreviation standing for “Special Containment Procedures”. This is mostly within a secret lab so locations arent a thing of interest here, instead the entities and objects are what are interesting. They can be harmless things to the worst horrors imaginable. Ill just drop the most famous scp here.

Out of the two I personally prefer the backrooms because I feel it has more scope and the general idea of the backrooms existing is very appealing for me. And I enjoy survival horror so yeah that too :p .
