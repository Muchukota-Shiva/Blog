Isn't everything a meme if you think about it?

BAAHAHHAHAHAHA I LOVE MEMES I LOVE MEMES I LOVE MEMES

Who does not like being happy?(note - suicidal people) Everybody wants to be happy and what is the best way of expressing happiness than laughing? Humor can make a person laugh and what is the best way of spreading humor.

Even besides humor this has become a very important thing in my life. First off it is a big part of internet culture. Follow this YouTube channel called lessons in meme culture to know what I mean. Literally anything happening world wide will be turned into a meme. As of writing the Ukraine-Russia situation, “the slap” and many more things have been memed. I dont event open news sites these days, I see a meme about something and then look up what happened as this piques my interest more.

Besides this we all know one of the most important things for friendship is being able to talk on something. Through memes I have been able to meet and have meaningful(???) conversation with countless people. Not sure if this is a sign or something but people outside of India seem to be more into meme culture and all cuz sometimes the memes I see are just not understood by Indians.

Rant begin

This brings me to a small subtopic. Indian memes. Its a sad scene. You dont have a lot of good stuff here. Meme templates are extremely overused. A meme never dies in the heads of indians and they keep repeating it even after it stopped being funny. Also i dont know if its just me but indian memes are too long, they keep you waiting for the next sentence or the next joke too long making me wanna go the next meme.

Rant over.

Memes have now become a part of my personality. Similar to Twomad, a streamer, I cant do things without referring to a meme or just cracking a joke in general. Some see this nicely(those who know) and some don’t(those who dont know). Now this is perfectly fine as I respect people’s opinions. But now this means that if people dont see this side of me nicely they will probably not like me at all so i generally hide my love for memes. BUT once some one finds it >:)

SO yeah make memes, love memes. Spread the memes spread the love.
