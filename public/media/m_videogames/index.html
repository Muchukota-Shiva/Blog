Well that was a rough start. anyways, Lets have a little history lesson. When did video games start to get commercially available? In 1976 a small company by the name of atari decided to do something when the tv isnt able to broadcast things i.e. some sort of offline entertainment using tv. They wanted it interactive too. SO they developed a game visible to use in a video format, A VIDEO GAME. This was an instant hit. Both kids and grown ups were loving it. But this was the start. What did really spread it’s influence? THE ARCADES! OMFG the arcades were an era I will miss them so much. The vibe of that place. People just waanna have fun. I remember going with change in my pocket, ready to spend it all out on the arcades. One of my best experiences in the arcade was when i was in 2nd or 3rd grade, the local pizza hut had street fighter 4. The waiter there would play with me and teach me how to play different characters. AAh those were the days :')

so yeah these were back when technological limitations would limit there from being something big for example - Pacman Watch this game theory video to know how deep it is even with technical limitations. This was a game with a tiny color palette and a sound and memory card in mbs. Still it was an influential hit and will remain a classic. These guys if would overcome the technical limitations could do so much! I cant quote everything but i will quote some of the significant ones(or atleast the ones i feel were significant)

I’ll start with the playstation. The console considered to be the og by many. Not the first to be 3d in the home consoles and was coming in after the arcades which were leagues ahead. BUT this platform gave one notable individual, Hideo Kojima. A chance to express himself to a greater extent. Earlier on the snes(an old console capable of putting out 16 bit graphics) made a little game called metal gear. While most games were about going and doing the action, our man realized his thoughts were too complex to be put on the device. So he made a game about sneaking from them, the birth of the stealth genre! The same action thrill but instead of shooting your way through you were sneaking your way through! A simple solution that gave birth to a new genre.(no wonder he won the industry icon award). So when the playstation came with the option of a 3rd axis, he got to work. Now not only was there a 3rd axis, we also had better audio, video clarity and more space. So he put his soul into this project and it was a huge huge success. Around this time came 3 stealth games, MGS, tenchu and thief. Of all of these mgs was the biggest hit.

OK better graphics so? its just another action thing. No. this game has a good story, not just hollywood boom bam shit, with themes of society, the 7 sins, problems with the government, where society is headed etc (predicting how society would change with stuff like twitter and all where your statements can decide your fate and how the future is dangerous). Truly ahead of its time.

Besides this for certain genres, having a player be in control is a very good tool to utilize. Like in some adventure movies, say, when the character is being chased by some animals in a jungle. In the movie it can get predictable as we know the protagonist cant really die so he will probably climb a tree or make a super cool jump onto the other side. This is, entertaining yes but not the most engaging thing out there. In case of a video game you are not sitting on a couch and just screaming go go, you are the one running, you are the one making decisions and you are the one responsible for your own actions. This is EXTREMELY engaging. Having the weight of consequences be on the player changes the ball game altogether so it creates a whole new level of excitement and entertainment. This was action, another place where this is crucial is games with rich stories and characters. Having your personality and social persona in a game be shaped based on your actions lets you create your own story which is amazing. A great example is almost everything made by teltale games.
