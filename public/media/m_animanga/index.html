How I Got Into Animanga And Why I Am Glad I Did - Fri, Apr 12, 2024

If anyone calls me a weeb I will come to their home and touch them

those who read this and said “JapAni CArtOOn” go fuck yourself

with that out the way lets begin.

I always enjoyed reading stuff. From a young age while others kids would read geronimo stilton and that stuff i would be reading encyclopedias and other stuff. Plus I was big on cartoons too. I would be watching adventure time, regular show and all while kids saw chhota bheem. This would probably explain why I have niche tastes.

So we would have cable connection right? sometimes, some channels would randomly stop working. Yeah. Back when that was a thing. SO when the general cartoon network and all wont work I would shift to toonami. It would have some anime like dragon ball, naruto etc. These looked different than most cartoons I saw. I realized that these are a probably a genre or made by the same creator, i noticed that these had something in common. Fast forward a week or two, new tv plan or something, change in channels. Introducing animax. I had never seen this guy before. I asked my friends, none of their TVs had this channel. Strange i thought. Bacha tha jo movies aur cartoons dekhta tha to aesa kuch ander hi ander laga ki I was chosen by this channel or something lol.

Turns out animax was a channel with target audience of the ages 14+. I was barely 7 or 8. yeah. Mujhe us samai english bhi theekse nahi aati thi so i would just watch the animations. For some reason my parents were also like okay maybe its just a cartoon when they werent. They had mature themes and content. But as i said mujhe samajh nahi aaya.

Soon i understood these types of shows are called anime. I looked into my encyclopedia. Turns out these were adapted from books called manga. These were graphic novels. I would just read story books at that time, not good enough to read big books. So i thought novels dur ki baat hai lets just leave it for now. But I learned something new. And this was staying with me.

As most of you know, I am not super mainstream. I like to stay a bit away from the mainstream, be with the underdogs and do the underrated stuff and all. So i made it my mission to check out these whenever I can.

Go a bit more into the future. 6th or 7th class. I would come back home from basketball class and would bathe and sit infront of tv. This time was generally not the best time to see cartoons. So i would just switch on animax. Now that I had a good grasp on english so now I would be fine watching anime(fun fact : my english reading speed was improved by reading anime subtitles no cap lol). I started realizing that the stories here were generally just better. They had better characters with great character, the visuals were more entertaining to look at, the stories were well written and engaging. I would constantly be waiting for the next episode like this. And we had so many genres here, action , romance, comedy, horror. I started feeling that this was the only channel I needed. So i would oscillate between animax, comedy central(for impractical jokers, the office, south park etc.) and the infotainment channels like natgeo, hitsorytv18 etc.

I would watch other channels so that I dont become a weeb. OH MAN I DIDNT WANNA BECOME A WEEB. Proudly I still am not one. I can make an entire thing about weebs. But another thing stopped me. Animax was removed from india. End of an era. They removed it cuz no extra profits. What a dick move. This is how i turned to the internet to get back to peak fiction.

Once the I tapped into the internet I realized animax was just the tip of the ice berg. THERE IS SO MUCH OUT THERE. And thats when i got into the real shit. THE MANGAS!

Mangas are the source material for anime. They take those panels and animate it. But i am they guy who says books are better than movies/adaptation

These were an excpetion for most of the part. These were damn good adaptions. But still, ab mai bada hogaya, parents said i cant watch cartoons. So i started reading the manga. Reading is productive right so i got the pass. Thats when I realized mangas are dope. You get just what the author wanted you to see. No fillers, no changes in plot, no bullshit. And these were sometimes better than the adaptions(eg. record of ragnorok. A fighting anime should be well animated, sadly this anime is a ppt). Also this made me find more content cuz not all mangas have been adapted, so number of mangas is more, and thats good cuz i am manga guy now. More stuff for me now.

This plus lockdown. OH MAN, how much i have red during lockdown. In my house there was some shitty rule by my parents to restrict gadget usage after 9th class. So i could barely watch or read animanga. BUT during lockdown ooh mama on pc almost all day. This nigga binged.

NOW PROS AND CONS OF ME GETTING INTO THIS.

firstly the pros - peak entertainment, not just masti stuff here. stuff with deep and dark themes that brought personality changes in me. Knowing animanga is a great thing to make friends. if you like some common anime or manga congrats you can be great friends now,

now the cons - society tumpe ek ganda sa thhappa dal deta hai XD. cartoon dekhne vaala. these guys will always hear anime and say either cartoon or hentia. bruh see the real stuff not all that but hey cant help it. Some guys get consumed and make this their life(weebs) and thats sad. I am no longer enjoying other cartoons cuz this shit is too good and cant be replaced.

THE BIGGEST PRO AND CON is the community. You will find all kinds of people here. Positive and negative. The community can be a big factor for any fandom. I dont have to elaborate on this you know what i mean oof

I feel everyone should at some point read or watch atleast one animanga. This is a big thing now. LOL i like how this is becoming mainstream but I am happy too cuz now everyone can experience this atleast in some way or the other.

I will make a recommendations post for animanga like i will for games so check that out too ;)
