The Weekly Run For Samosas - Sun, Jul 12, 2026

Agra samosas had us acting up for real

The pole incident took place in air force school Agra, these shenanigans are from there too.

You might say what was up with the school, well it was even built weird. It was located on a one way road. Yeah the busses would drop us at the gate of the school which was on the T junction. Then when the school would be over the bus would come from the opposite direction so that they can directly pick us up and leave. So it was just one direction for the vehicles. On one side of the road was the primary school and on the other side was the secondary school. So there was a clear divide. Now for some backstory.

The T junction would be where the normal main road for the air force station would be, but the other side where the vehicles would come from was near the air base. Now we were 4th and 5th graders. The word air base kinda gave us classified vibes, we would look down that road, mostly empty but every now and then an official jeep or bike would go by without stopping. We however would hide from them. It was not allowed for us primary students to go to the senior side. The only times we were allowed were for dance/music classes or special assemblies and even then we were queued in lines and monitored by our teachers. If we were ever caught, well it was so over.

The junior side of the school has a giant wall to keep us little rascals from breaking out. The senior side has a lower wall, so low you could vault over, but i wont complain lol. Anyways a giant wall would be in our way, and if a small kid was seen trying to get across anyhow teachers would catch the child and call their parents.

But why try going out? The primary side of the school was not small by any means. We had a big park, a football and basketball court, lots of classrooms, 2 computer labs blah blah. So why? Was the school so bad that kids would try running out? I just said we would hide whenever a vehicle passed by then why were we so desperate to go out? Well there is one thing the primary side did not have.

A school canteen.

What do kids love? not eating the food they got in their lunch boxes and gobbling up snacks that’s what. But there were no snacks, all you could do was trade food with your friends but if everyone had just bhindi roti or cold maggi cake you would start craving other things.

One day news spread in our school. Some kid whose elder brother was in the secondary school told us that there is a canteen on their side of the school. We were torn. How could our school forsake us like this. Did we not deserve yummy stuff at a marginal price too? Whatever we were kids and we didn’t have stuff like depression back then. We quickly started thinking of how we can get that stuff. First answer was to ask that guys brother to bring us the stuff. The guy wont be stopped from crossing the road to meet his younger brother right? Well the guy straight up refused. Fair tho i wont go around carrying junk for small kids (▰˘◡˘▰) .

So we knew what had to be done. We would go to the canteen. How though? We had to sneak out when there was a crowd, when monitoring is a bit lax and when the canteen might be open. Sounds like lunch time. So one day of the week we decided to bring to school whatever money we needed for snacks and made a plan, with paper, sketch pens and everything. We made a little map with positions, formations everything.

We chose the bravest kid to go risk his ass in the wild. The others would keep lookout around places where teachers would often be there. A few would look towards the road to look for vehicles.

Patiently we waited for our turn, eyes peeled, sweat trickled down our backs with heat and stress. Finally, the sports teacher went to fill her bottle. A kid went on all fours, the brave heart stepped on his back and pulled himself up the wall, and he was out! We dared not to cheer, would be suspicious. A few of us peeked out to see how he was. I still remember watching him sprint to the other side. He did not look back. Conveniently he ran in a straight line which was right near the canteen.

Silence. We did not see him for a while. How we longed for a walkie talkie. What if he got caught? What if the canteen guy snitched? What if he got injured by chance? We were tense, butt clenched. My friend pulled me away from the wall so as to not raise suspicion. I started eating my tiffin. A few minutes passed. We were loosing hope.

Soon, my friend came running to me “oye, wo aagaya”. I left my lunchbox and ran out. There he was with an oily newspaper full of samosas. The way we all erupted with joy. Random risky shenanigan in broad daylight. The samosas tasted better once the stress was gone. We gobbled up whatever was in front. We also let the runner have some extra cuz you know boy risked his ass for a snack for all.

“tooooo, agle hafte fir?”. We did this almost every week for the next month or two. The runner changing every now and then. I never got a chance to be the runner even though i was the fastest runner, as school just ended lmao. But i dont regret it. It was all fun anyways.

I feel shenanigans like this help shape your personality a lot. Makes you courageous and helps build a bond with people. Yeah its badmaashi but fuck that :p

p.s the canteen guy never snitched haha. He getting more business so I guess he played along.
