# **Context** {.text-center}

I grew up in quite a strict house. Stuff like no meeting friends after 8pm, only an hour of internet in a week during 9th and 10th class and all that shabang. So the idea of ME drinking ALCOHOL is blasphemous. Even more so considering that both of my parents do not drink daily, they also try not to drink during gatherings. So it is obvious that they taught me not to drink as well. 

Side tangent - I am not very keen on drinking as well. India's drinking culture isnt very good. In most countries where people drink a lot it is either due to the place being stupid cold and you need warmth from the alcohol OR that they need it for medicinal qualities(I don't know much about this but i have heard this quite a few times). People in India however drink mostly "to go talli". It happens in other countries too but over there they atleast drink properly, you should not drink on an empty stomach. People there finish their meal and have a drink however in india people just tear open a pack of aloo bhujia with some chopped onions and go ham on their liver.
I would not mind drinking but I have low tolerance and go from tipsy to drunk quite easily. Considering that and how my friends are too I usually did not feel safe enough to drink with them, cuz one time in bangalore[explained in incident 2] i was happy n drunk but the buzzkill around me got me sober so fast I lowkey had to drag and carry my folks home lol
anyways tangent over - 

Now my parents were strict but they were also very protective with me and my sister. While i appreciate it, it also meant that I did not get exposure to a lot of things till I like actually stepped out there into the bad place. My father tries to teach me the "why learn after getting trouble, why not learn before hand and avoid trouble in the first place" but I usually learn things after experiencing stuff cuz i'm curious like that :p
BUT the main point here is that, in college i was not under constant surveillance and i low key just discovered free will or something. 

So i did discover free will and very much am in control of my vices. However a few times, I let my guard down and agreed to have a drink, which led to quite some funny antiques. 


# **Incident** {.text-center}

So this was my first time drinking. This was the 2nd semester, I was the editorial head in ACM and we had successfully finished the annual event we conduct. So all core committee members were invited for dinner to one of the fancier restaurants in Bidholi. We arrived on time and ordered first round of food and all. Then I see some of the folks whispering amongst themselves, nishita didi gives me a sheepish smile and says "peene ke liye kya loge" and I see her pull out a bottle of whiskey. I see everyone looking at me eagerly ki like, ye kya bolega. So I was quite a formal person in that chapter, i used to do my work on time and do it well, keep gossip to a minimum(atleast with the core committee XD) and I usually would not go for outings with them for various reasons. They were not exactly good people but thats a story for another time lol. 

Anyways I was in a good mood that day, and most of the folks in that core committee were from my campus hostel itself so I thought the responsibility would be shared with them for me to return safely. So I let them know this would be my first time drinking please like treat me well n shit and if i say stuff ignore it. I think shlok bhaiyya went all, "sala ye to daaru virgin hai isko to pilaenge" and the plan was set in motion ki aaj hoga full daaru panti.

A glass with some amount of whiskey (idk how much) and some coca cola was mixed and given to me. After eating our starters they all waited for me to take the first sip. I took a sip and went bleh but full crowd cheered haha. I kept sipping a lil and could feel myself going a lil tipsy. So instead of dialing it back I decided to walk around a lil and see what others are up to. Folks were dancing and i was getting groovy with them. I was even swearing at them without remorse XD. but soon something happened which changed my trajectory for the night. 

Chahat came trotting along, yapping about some how she is proud that I drank and shit whatever. something something happens but she just hands me her glass of neat whiskey with ice. My dumbass went bottoms up on that shit. 

And like i lowkey forgot what happened for the next hour or so lol. The only details I know from that doozy period was what I heard others, and boy did i hear some stuff. First account was that they saw me dance better than I danced in my flash mob. I was dancing with everyone regardless of my relation with them. I was even swearing at people regardless of their relation with them. 

Besides this one major incident was that I somehow climbed one of the trees??? and the staff had to come bring me down :sob: which is honestly hilarious I wish i remembered the details of it would have been amazing. The closest detail to this that we have rn is after i was escorted down the tree I just laid down and then everyone took a group photo which is ROFL.

The other issue was that i started posting on my story (shoutout if you one of the few people who saw it before it was deleted). One of the people there saw that I am recording something so she quickly came and deleted my story. Shashank, good friend, saw my story from hostel and called paridhi out of concern, and she also understood the situation. She quickly spoke to me and said shiva no more recording and all. My friends were trying to call me but she took my phone and was just telling the few people who saw my story before deletion that he is ok and will be brought home safely. 

Besides this, it was about to be 8pm which was the curfew. Oh shit oh fuck. Most guys were getting somewhat sober but not me, i was as high as a kite. So a plan was made to do tripply on one of the bikes. One of the drinkers with good tolerance who was sober will drive, another semi-sober guy will be in the back, and then there will be me sandwiched in the middle. I was A-Ok in the start but as the guy started driving a lil fast I started panicking, and knowing bidholi has a hilly terrain i was getting more angsty. So i started screaming on top of my lungs ki like "arrre baba mala zaoede" and "bike roke warna mai tumhari gaand mai moot dunga" and stuff lol. imagine a bullet going 40-50 kmph at 8pm on a dark road with some lad screaming passing by.

Now for one of the final hurdles. Getting back into the hostel. Our warden is quite strict, an ex-army guy. Thankfully my entire gang had already arrived at the college gate concerned. My ACM colleagues handed me over to my gang and lowkey said take care of him and stuff. Firstly my friends kinda had fun with me cuz this was the first time they saw me in a state like that.  A few friends were beyond disapointed that I of all people decided to drink so they didnt come till the gate but the rest who came were having the time of their life. Trivia - it was on this very moment that I changed my ring tone to kanye west's dark fantasy with the lines "can we get much higher, so high-igh". - next my friend were kinda giving me a shoulder and helping me reach hostel.

A plan was made that I will stay shut and the folks will do the talking. The plan was that I will do my signature, and then they will just say isko khelte khelte chot lag gayi ye sick bay jaa raha hai. Works works. We entered the guard room, i did my in time and attendance while shashank who was friends with gaurd was doing small talk. I for some god known reason made the raspberry lip sound while signing. The entire room went quiet for a second or two, but thankfully romanch saved me and said "haan haan khana millega thhodi der mai thhand rakh" and the gaurd laughed it off. Phew I MADE IT IN. And thankfully the warden didnt find me or something cuz annirudha had told the warden he is sick and will skip lunch, he already had fruits and is resting.

Oh wait ... annirudha. He would have skinned me alive if he knew i went and drank. Its a little ironic now that he drinks and smokes now but whatever back then he was holding himself well together. So annirudha comes storming to my room, he very firmly and lowkey rudely makes everyone leave my room saying let this guy have some space let him be. He asked me to lock up and sleep in his room so he can monitor me and stuff cuz my current roommate was a druggy and unreliable. I reached his room, called my parents and confessed. Mom and shria were dissapointed, but papa was like >"fine you tried it but dont do it again" which is good advice. 

Whatever next he gave me lots of water and said just go to sleep. I lowkey slept well that day. I did not drink again till my 4th year till i went to bangalore where I had to drink with office colleagues for you know corporate reasons :rolling_eyes: 

But I will still remember this day, cuz this was one of the "firsts" you can experience in life.
