Has this ever happened to you before? Does not matter if you ever the person on the left or right. You cant say no.

SO what is going on here? We humans have a very deep and complex brain, and to add to it each person has a unique brain. Meaning that we all think and feel differently. This will naturally lead to conflict as we believe/accept what others are thinking as what I think is supreme and what they think is obviously wrong, right?

Now this will be a hard pill to swallow but we live in a society, a social world. Everything is relative and subjective. What you think might be right in someone's eyes but downright wrong in someone else's eyes. The vice-versa is true too. 

Opinion wars not always end well because sometimes people are stubborn, and very idealistic. People really really like sticking with their ideas, nobody is willing to budge. These can either be harmless like "oh too bad" or just might straight up ruin your relations with people. Example a friend of mine was a die hard, die hard fan of this guy on youtube I forgor the details of it. I personally felt his content was way too childish even to like ironically, one day while he showed it to me I was seriously disinterested and it looked like it too. So he got quite offended for I was not interested in something i didnt like. I let him know it was childish and not great content aaaand this unfortunately resulted in him loosing a friend in me. Now that I look back at it there was a better way to deal with this.

Step 1 - learn to accept that you are not always correct and your opinion is not supreme or something. Shoutout to BTS stans for being a bad example of this. They are not willing to accept a "I like xyz singer" where xyz =/= Korean music band. In this case accept that taste in music is subjective as there is no definitive way of saying this is the better song/singer. If someone else likes something else(again perfectly normal thing) think to yourself "ok thats fine" because it is! 

step 2 - If you really want to say that you like something more just say "I like xyz more than abc" instaed of "xyz is better than abc". Can you see the difference here? One appreciates the other person's opinion while the other straight up says that the other person's taste/thinking is wrong which may or may not be true.


I feel this is one of the reasons I am very amiable and affable. I do not go against people's opinions and am a good listener, no matter how dumb or weird a person's opinion is I still calmly listen to it and say I FEEL SO instead of IT IS SO

Well this is all I can say, its in your head. You cant change everyone so why not just change yourself? I am not asking you to go against your ideals. Me saying that would be very hypocritical as I am quite stubborn about my moral ideals but have a stand where it is needed, not where you feel so ;p
