I LOVE HAVING FRIENDS. yes i am a somewhat lonely ass omnivert but i still love hanging out with them. I like can not live without them. "but shiva you are lowkey an introvert you should be fine being alone". Yes, i like my me times. Sit back, listen to songs, read manga, drink some joos, meditate, study. Fr thats all i think i really like doing alone. The funny thing is i like doing the above mentioned things with friends too!

Also I often choose not to interact with people who I feel are not worth being around, so often my inactivity socially is out of choice. Think of it like this; if you are having pizza and there is no proper toppings around, would you put honey on it just because it is the only thing available at the moment? No right? Just like that I also do not get close with just any body because they are the only people around. 

Me liking being around people can prolly be due to my love for psychology and curiosity to know what other people are like, how are they different from me, any similarities here and there etc. Also cuz i was used to having many friends before i became more introverted than my younger self but i guess the earlier reason was better.

Now people are nice and all, but not all people are your friends. This was a tough pill for an overly optimistic person like me. To most people out there you are just a tool, cuz life is a race and everyone is trying to get forward but not everyone tries to play fair always. 

Plus there are many people who are just straight up nasty :| for whatever reason. Others i get it they wanna do well in life but why be nasty to me man :| I am always nice to others so probably was nice to you too. 

So the important thing is finding people who like you for who you are as a person, not people who like you for what you have/what you can give them.

This-is-not-an-easy-task. People are devious. They will put in you in a false sense of friendship, get work done from you under the pretext of friendship and then act like they have never met you. Now this doesn't mean you just start ignoring people and are always overthinking like "wait is this person actually nice or just trying to get stuff from me.". This was a big mistake on my side, for this made me come across as a cold minded and closed selfish person. DUe to this i also lost some friends on the long run for i was not able to see that they were genuinely nice and not screwing with me.

It took me some time to realize this but this behavior of mine is what was the opposite of what I follow. Help others but dont expect anything in return. I know I know this also meant dont be a donkey and get everything taken from you but isnt this what a man needs for peace? Once you start expecting things in return you constantly think about whether you will get what you wanted in return or not. And you know when the mind games begin you are fucked. 

Again all i expect is some friendship in return but also not everyone will be a true friend of yours so whats best is to have everyone in good terms with you. Your friends will love you regardless of who you are, your acquaintances will be with you as long as you have some form of contact(anything like meeting in class, seeing each other on the way to grab a drink etc.) and this rep of your will give you a positive reputation for the general public too. 

I went off topic. Again a true friend is much more precious than a bunch of acquaintances. Which is why i generally have a small friend circle of really close friends and the rest are just acquaintances. BUT i wanna change this. I will try to get more close friends. 

As of writing my second year is gonna start in two months or so and with that I will get a new batch of students. My aim is to get a decent amount(cant define decent but i guess it means more than the amount of good friends i had in my first year batch[about 5-7 only from 55-60 kids]) . To do this I will work on my posture and body language so that i dont come across as that one kid who only studies and does not wish to socialize(will take help from my wonderful extrovert sis and probably r/socialskills XD). In addition to this i will also change my attire a little, currently looking like too much of that padhai shadhai waala baalak :| will get some more casuals to make sure my goofy side is seen too.

So yeah, I will work on getting more friends and make sure that everyone sees my friendly and amiable side too :)
