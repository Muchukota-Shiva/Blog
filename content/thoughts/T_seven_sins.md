Now, the seven deadly sins has always been an interesting concept to me. I admire it like I admire the 9 layer's of hell shown in Dante's Inferno. They are a negative things but are oddly interesting. 

We all have at least one of these in us. If you say no you are an ignorant fuck. Actually we have all of these in us, just the amount varies. Cope. These seven sins are pride, envy, sloth, lust, wrath, greed, gluttony. 

Now lets start with what sins I had and have more or less control.

First the one I am most proud of overcoming, wrath! I was a very violent and outgoing kid when I was younger. Till my 8th class or so If someone flipped my switch they were in for trouble. I have gone so far as hurting some really good friends of mine brutally which I still regret today. The regret from that still makes me think twice and thrice before thinking of hitting someone. When I was going towards my 9th grade I got into Buddhism and started thinking more responsibly, and started reflecting on my actions(even better i used to do it with guts theme). This somehow magically turned me into my monk like self today which I am very happy about :) I am very happy to report that in the past 5 years I have been a very happy and calm individual who wouldn't lay a finger on anyone no matter how grim the situation got. Hoping it stays so.

Second lets get onto greed. I was a very competitive kid. Or so I thought. I was an attention seeker. I used to like getting praised, this led me to developing greed. I wanted everything, If I had everything I would be praised right? This made me develop a tendency of greed. I was never content with what I had. Again around 9th or 10th I started realizing the value of things and started appreciating my own limits. I started feeling thankful for what I had and started thinking less about what I don't have. Soon before I knew it my greed was replaced with gratitude.

The third is closely tied to greed, envy. Being an attention seeker was a terrible thing. Seeing someone else get attention and not me !??! heresy. This tendency of mine went away again as I started appreciating others around me. This required me to kick my ego down. Also reduced my sin of pride(more on this later). 

Man I really went through some renaissance or something after my 8th class huh XD

The fourth is the controversial sin, Lust. Now I have been quite the bhola bacha all my life(people say I still am and I like to ignore that) and have stayed away from all things you associate lust with. I would try distancing myself from people who would talk about it and try to be in my own bubble where all this was not there. Around 10th end and 11th start my bubble burst and this teen was suddenly exposed to god knows what not. I kind of went berserk for a while but then this happened during my w o k e period so my moral compass jumped in and steered me away. Soon it kicked me that I am being useless by thinking all this so with sheer will power I got over it. This is a good thing cuz I respect woman as individuals now and not as objects as I see most kids my age do so. 

fifth is gluttony. Uhhh i like junk food but I prefer the healthy alternative whenever I can. Also I am skinny bruh you think Iam a glutton lol. 

Now. NOW. Lets talk about what I could not.

 Starting with what I am currently battling. The lion's sin pride. I take pride in being a good person, being a reliable person, being someone who you can turn to. Also some little talents or whatever I have. People say rightfully so, I am quite a rare and unique person in this modern society. But still pride is pride. I have kicked my superiority complex out of the crib but there still is some residue left in me. Wanting to help people out or improve them gives me a sense of empowerment which I always try to suppress. I am a very humble person and am a very ground to earth person who respects others but sometimes I feel I have pride. Why did I mention this? of all sins mentioned above, this one is relatively more so in me so I dropped it here. If you are feeling kind enough you might say I dont have pride and I appreciate that :) *realizes his pride is trying to make him ignore it bruh*

Lastly. One sin which I never had but recently contracted. SLOTH. I am lazy as fuck boi. This is one place where my moral compass cant stop me. I like taking shortcuts. I work hard but man I am lazy no cap. As said by some dude on reddit **"Sloth is an excessive laziness or the failure to act and utilize one’s talents."** yes.  This to a certain extent gives me a defeatist attitude and is probably a reason why some people call me "submissive". Any tips to work on this will be appreciated 0y0


So yeah cheers on the sins conquered, lets get the others to a minimum too ;)
