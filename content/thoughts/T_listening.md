Ever felt like when you are talking to some and you feel man this guy just wont listen to me. Probably in some other conversation the guy must be like damn this mf wont stop. This is because a conversation has two parts. First is obviously speaking, the second is listening. Believe it or not the second is more important. Think of this like a game of catch. You throw a ball, the other guy catches it. Catching the ball is very important because if you keep throwing the ball and no one catches it, you are going nowhere. 

Great if it is important how do i do it. Patience. Thats the first step. You will get a chance to speak too. Let the other person give his/her views and then continue. This way you understand what the other person is up-to and also make that person feels involved in the conversation(who does not like having someone to talk to). 

Next try to be an 'active listener'. Dont hear, listen. Give the person proper feedback like a nod or a subtle mmhhmm. Also absorb what they are speaking. Don't just think dang it's gonna be my turn to speak. NO understand what the guy has to say. 

Now this does not mean silence. I agree meaningful silence is better than meaningless words but comparing to our first example this is like just standing and catching while not throwing back, whats the fun in that!

I am a good listener and i can say this is very helpful in general. You understand people better, people enjoy talking more to you(and i enjoy being talked to) and you come out as a calmer person too.

So listen, be a good listener
